Mini Project 3 Part 2: Fruit Basket

This is a group project in collaboration with my groupmates, Aron F and Ryan N. The project uses pixel art images to create unique images. The main theme we chose is still life, specifically fruits. All pieces are in a 20x20 scale. I made pineapple and grape, which are my two favorite fruits, and I like the colors. On the main page, I briefly introduce my mini project, my team members, and the two arrays that I made in part 1. The first page shows my 2 arrays in different versions, such as monochrome, color, and text. I really like this page because it shows many states of the original arrays; it is different and unique. On the second page is a new creation, I can change it as I want. I used triangles and adjusted them in different directions to apply to my arrays. Additionally, I used other shapes to apply to my remaining arrays. On the last page, I used my teammate's design. I liked their two designs, Aron chose apple and Ryan chose blueberry, it made me feel interested and impressed that they arrayed the details and shading of the fruit. I used my bitmaps to apply to their arrays. Luckily, it worked beautifully and exactly as I intended. I created bitmaps on two different themes, one about the ocean, where there are shrimps, fish, and boats, and one about weather, like flowers representing beautiful weather, rain, snowmen, and stars.

The difficult thing about this mini project is that I had to arrange all the numbers in the right position so that it could create the image I wanted. Next, I had to rely on it to make the text array, which was very difficult and took a lot of time to adjust. My success was that I achieved a satisfactory result. This was the biggest collaboration that I had done with my teammates; they helped and supported me a lot. I have enjoyed working on this project and our teammates.
