Excercise 2

This exercise was difficult for me because I did not have a clear idea of what I wanted to do from the beginning. The chessboard is inspired by two strong contrasting colors, white and black. I encountered many issues when practicing this work. Many new knowledge that I thought I was good at it but maybe not, I have to spend a lot of time reviewing and practi cing to make them make sense. I struggled with the toggle function, I did not understand how it worked, and I asked my classmate for help explaining it again! Thanks a lot, I understood and applied it to my work. I used the image of a white chess piece to control with ‘a,w,s,d’ keys, to move it all over a chessboard. A toggle is applied to shake the white piece and turn it on/ off by pressing ‘p and x’. A black chess piece is set to be in a random position, every time you refresh, the position will always change. Applying images and fonts has created many changes in the work. The success that I see and love in my work is I made two chess pieces interact with each other, they touch each other, tint is applied to create drama when they touch, and “Boom!.” This is the final version that I did, and that makes me satisfied so far. Project 1

This is an impressive project for me because I like and enjoy the way it works, I can move the mouse to make my work and save them. This is an interesting project, I can be creative in many different ways when drawing on the canvas with six different brushes. I created six brushes and they will be selected when pressing from 1 to 6. The brushes are created by different strokes and geometric shapes combined with different colors to create variety and novelty. If you are bored with the white canvas, you can also change to a black canvas when pressing the ‘n’ key, which makes the project interesting. I have changed and combined the brushes together to be able to create many different images. The success of this project is that I have learned how to create my own paint template, using unique brushes to be able to create images.

The difficulty for me is that I had not yet shaped which brush I wanted to create, I have to spend some time to consider what I like to put in my code. There were many issues when I did it, which I should review and edit the details so they did not interfere with each other. I had fun creating a lot of work and chose the 12 images that were best for me and four that stood out to me.
